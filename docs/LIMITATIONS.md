# Limitations

- REL v0.1 operates on structured observations; it does not solve natural-language state inference.
- Human state is only represented to the extent relevant evidence is supplied.
- High representation fidelity does not guarantee ethical behavior or safe action.
- A user's expressed preference may conflict with external safety requirements.
- The current metrics are reference metrics, not validated psychometric instruments.
- The current implementation is not a clinical or psychological diagnostic system.
- Historical oscillator and bio-physiological hypotheses are conceptual lineage, not dependencies of the AI/ML package.
- Benchmark evidence against production AI systems is future work.
