# Prior Art and Research Boundary

REL overlaps with established areas and should be evaluated against them rather than presented as if they do not exist.

Relevant fields include:

- user modeling and personalization;
- preference learning and value learning;
- Theory of Mind and belief-state modeling;
- epistemic logic and multi-agent belief representation;
- inverse planning / inverse reinforcement learning;
- affective computing;
- uncertainty calibration;
- human feedback and AI alignment;
- corrigibility and intent alignment.

## What is established

Maintaining user models, representing beliefs/preferences, calibrating uncertainty, and separating agent beliefs are not new problems.

## REL contribution being tested

REL packages these concerns around a specific safety thesis: **human-alignment quality depends on preserving evidential boundaries between explicit human state, model inference, uncertainty, and model/world knowledge before action selection.**

Potential value lies in the combination, failure taxonomy, metrics, and evaluation protocol—not in claiming invention of every underlying technique.

## Research requirement

Any future novelty claim should be tied to a concrete mechanism or empirical result and checked against current literature before publication.
