# REL Conceptual Origins

Earlier REL work developed from a bio-physiological and cross-system view of connection. Three recurring concepts were:

1. **Structural reconstruction** — one system reconstructs relevant structure from another system's observable signals.
2. **Temporal relation** — interpretation occurs across an interaction window rather than as an isolated snapshot.
3. **Sustained coherence** — representation and interaction remain sufficiently consistent above noise to support functional understanding.

The Bio-Physiological Architecture also emphasized interaction sensitivity, signal quality, jitter/drift, and the difference between surface imitation and grounded structural understanding.

## Translation into this repository

The public AI-safety implementation carries forward only what can be stated conservatively and operationalized:

- structural reconstruction becomes evidence-backed human-state representation;
- temporal relation motivates context-aware evaluation but not a public longitudinal-intelligence engine;
- sustained coherence becomes consistency among evidence, representation, uncertainty, and response;
- interaction sensitivity becomes confidence/evidence weighting;
- surface imitation is explicitly separated from representation fidelity.

The deeper generalized asymmetry and longitudinal relational-computation work is outside the scope of this open-source repository.
